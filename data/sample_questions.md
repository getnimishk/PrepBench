## Question 1
What is the primary role of the Scrum Master during Sprint Planning?
- Type: single_choice
- Difficulty: medium
- Domain: Agile & Scrum
- Topic: Scrum Roles
- Certification: PSM I - Professional Scrum Master

### Options
- [ ] Assigning tasks to each developer based on their capacity
- [x] Ensuring that the event takes place and attendees understand its purpose
- [ ] Determining the final list of Product Backlog items to include
- [ ] Approving the Sprint Goal defined by the Product Owner

### Explanation
The Scrum Master acts as a servant leader ensuring Scrum events take place, are productive, and kept within the timebox.
